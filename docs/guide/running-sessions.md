# Running sessions

## Starting

You start a session from a world — open the world and click **Begin session**. A short wizard then walks three steps:

1. **World** — pick from your saved worlds (a companion cast member gives you someone to play opposite from the first beat; forge one if the cast is thin).
2. **Embodiment** — *Play a character* (you act in-world; optionally pick which library character you are) or *Observer* (you direct from outside). The selected card is highlighted with a check.
3. **Title** — defaults to the world name.

Spawning copies the world into session state: locations, links, participants, worn item instances, seeded story threads. From there the play screen streams turns; the sidebar shows participants, scene, clock, wardrobe and containers.

## Scene images

Generated every N turns (session setting, adjustable in the Scene tab), on director flag, or manually. Images are shot from your first-person point of view — you never appear in them. The focal character is chosen from whoever shares the room with you, and every outfit comes from the *recorded wardrobe state*, never from prose.

## Deleting

On the world page's Sessions list, the ✕ button deletes a session after a confirm — this permanently removes its play history (turns, memories, facts and scene images); the world and characters are untouched. Once a world has no sessions, the world itself can be deleted.
