## Verdict

The decision, first, in one or two sentences. Pass, fail, or pass-with-limits —
and what happens to the flag or the feature as a result.

## What we tried

The question the trial was set up to answer, and how it was put to the test, at
the level of "we ran N conversations and looked for X".

## What a player would notice

The player-visible difference, good and bad. Concrete observations, not metrics.

## Limitations

What this trial did not cover, and which claims it therefore cannot support.

## Next steps

What happens now — enable, revise, re-run, or abandon — and which plan or slice
owns that work.
